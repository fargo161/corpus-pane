CORPUS PANE / CHECKPOINT 02.2.5
A growing anatomical library with explicit perspective knowledge.
Start: Open two examples or restore your corpus backup. The same universal skeleton is used for every compatible character source.
Correct: Click a pane, choose a group, then open Skeleton & face. Drag points; Alt+click places a missing point. Hide parts that are not visible. Enlarge the face and adjust its five slots and oval. Undo keeps changes reversible.
Refine: Select the group and frame range. Refine proposes fresh tracking using manual references; it does not apply until you choose an acceptance button. Your pins and protected points cannot be replaced by the pass.
Compare: Pause each clip independently. Search for a similar pose in the other clip. Different motions need not share frame numbers. A length or angle difference is an observation to explain, not proof that a skeleton failed.
Verify: Check the selected group’s positions and visibility, then Verify & protect. Only that group and range are approved. Fixing a verified point later makes any affected approval or reusable reference stale.
Reuse: Confirm the viewing direction and size guides, then name a verified configuration. Find checked references searches these saved configurations as you add more clips. Match cards now say whether the requested region is fully covered or only partially covered. Rough query matches are visibly labeled and remain suggestions. It does not retrain the detector or copy a different character’s measurements.
Save: Browser autosave is convenient, not your only backup. Save backup creates a ZIP with source videos, separate JSON records, tracks, decisions and references. Import merges safely; conflicting clip revisions are kept separately.
Add more: Use Add video / frames, or attach the original corpus ZIP. Earlier clips remain in the library. Loading new sources does not require a new program build.
This is a single-figure, 2D verification prototype. Whole-character segmentation of arbitrary backgrounds, final cutout-part harvesting, rig retargeting and guaranteed automatic occlusion are not claimed. The shared skeleton remains independent of the canonical still mannequin.
Perspective knowledge: Record body and head direction separately. For the selected region, describe whether it is ordinary, foreshortened, edge-on, crossing the body, folded, ambiguous, or hidden. These are 2D observations, not a solved 3D body.
Knowledge: The new Knowledge tab explains why the program asks for these records and surfaces the most relevant articles for the current body region. It never turns a rule into verification by itself.
