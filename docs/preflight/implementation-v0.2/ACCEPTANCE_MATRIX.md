## Preserved quality targets and measurement rules

- Pose: median≤2% image height and p95≤5% against owner-reviewed anatomical-side-aware references. Gate 2B models did not establish this; report raw provider and corrected outputs separately. Correction can make an authoring result usable without pretending raw model passed.
- Hair0.75 and garment0.80 remain **coarse diagnostic warning levels**, not certified fine-boundary acceptance. SAM seed0 hair0.7192/dress0.8768 on sparse coarse references cannot close hair/accessory/occlusion quality. U08 labels/V02 trials required before final metrics are approved.
- Real garment:25 specified shoulder/elbow poses, normalized height1024, seam/alignment≤2px, no inversion, intentional overlap and reviewed draw order. No smaller pose set substituted to pass.
- Face: structural residual≤1% reviewed interocular distance; second identity/kit real proof and native mask behavior required.
- Pair: contact error≤2% normalized height, timing≤1 native frame plus coherent visual interaction on compatible standardized females. Pixel overlap is not contact ground truth.
- Correction burden: record inspection/reseed/edit/rejection/export seconds, prompts, corrected frames and accepted usable output per minute. No measured budget currently exists; first owner-reviewed sessions establish a proposed budget for approval, not retroactive lowered quality targets.
- Runtime: reproducible named hardware/config, cold load versus warm p50/p95 and total job time, sample counts, GPU allocator/reserved versus whole-device peak and process-tree RSS. New resource budgets are operability proposals and cannot override the preserved quality targets.

Tests bind results to exact versions; fresh reruns after code/input changes supersede applicability, not history. Any exception/limited release requires an explicit owner decision in DECISION_LEDGER; it cannot be described as full acceptance.
