## Common fixture/harness

Freeze before scoring: input PNG/source rig hashes; test IDs and calibration revision; normalized body height1024; anatomical-side/view/angle definitions; camera/source-to-canonical transforms; candidate code/profile/mapping hashes; dependency lock and hardware. Canonical pose targets are joint positions/orientations under skeleton1.2.0 semantics, not copied local Euler values from the old hierarchy. The control-to-canonical mapping must make the alternatives perform equivalent requested motion.

1. Neutral anatomical/visual reference: preserve exact original source bytes and a historical rendering where reproducible. New-rig neutral views are judged against reviewed anatomical anchors, artwork coverage and intent, not identical hierarchy or necessarily identical pixels. Record intended corrections and displacement; do not silently change the benchmark. Failure to reproduce the old topology is never a failure.
2. Existing25-pose arm/shoulder/torso grid: shoulder[-20,-10,0,10,20] × elbow[-30,-15,0,15,30], expressed as common anatomical target definitions. Seam/alignment target≤2px at normalized height1024; no inverted triangles, gaps in required joint coverage or invalid draw order. Do not shorten the grid to make an alternative pass.
3. Additional frozen stress fixtures: at least one hip/pelvis articulation, raised/crossed arm, head/neck/collar attachment and front/behind transition, plus donut/two-island/strap geometry. Derive test values from reviewed anatomy/available evidence and record support. These add evidence without claiming a full hip/lower-body production envelope from a sleeve test.
4. Modularity operations: independent hair, garment, accessory, HEAD_IDENTITY and five-slot kit changes with body-motion hash invariant. Use real reference art and clearly labelled synthetic alternatives where second assets are absent. Such results measure architecture mechanics, not U05/U07 real substitution acceptance.
5. Authoring tasks: import/map art; split a sleeve or joint panel; author seam overlap; adjust pivot independently of anatomy/socket; paint/repair weights; change order; add a variant; save/reopen. Same task definitions, operator experience disclosure, three timed repetitions after a documented practice pass; record intervention/undo/error counts.
6. Data integration: import a common source-backed canonical-surface fixture with mixed OBSERVED/RECOVERED/INFERRED/UNKNOWN regions and test contributor inspection, transforms, playback and export/reopen. Early fixture is prepared for the comparison, not claimed as M04 reconstruction proof. M09 repeats this with actual M04/M05 outputs.

## Hard gates before scoring

- 100% 2D; universal skeleton1.2.0 evidence/motion semantics preserved. Internal deform controls may differ but must not replace the canonical skeleton or blur anatomy, art pivots and sockets.
- Independent body/head/five-slot face/hair/garment/accessory binding; correct layered overlap/seam/order support; no provider or rig-native identity/acceptance authority.
- Exact historical source art/rig preserved and recoverable. New topology, splits, weights, pivots/sockets and renderer allowed only as versioned derivatives, with coverage origin and mapping evidence.
- Required25-pose seam/alignment≤2px, no inversion/degenerate accepted mesh or missing required coverage, valid order. If a prototype cannot meet the required envelope, mark failed/incomplete rather than hiding poses in a narrower envelope. Extra unsupported ranges can remain rejected without shrinking the inherited target.
- Default path has no mandatory unresolved/research-only dependency; manual/no-model fallback and declared resource caps work. No destructive canonical migration or acceptance promotion.

The full candidate need not be production complete; each mandatory test must identify PASS/FAIL/NOT_EVALUATED and scope. A candidate with an unmet hard gate cannot win on average score. Missing external real-head/pair evidence is shared as a later acceptance blocker, not incorrectly assigned as a prototype failure or treated as passed.

## Registered comparison criteria

Weights are proposed now and frozen before measurements. Score0–5 with anchors tied to recorded raw metrics and reviewed visual rubrics; total is sum(weight×score/5), maximum100. Before running, the Independent Tester freezes per-metric score bins and visual rubric anchors alongside the raw-unit measures; score bins cannot be chosen after seeing results. No aggregate score is valid with unmeasured weighted criteria. Disclose subjective reviewer scores and disagreement. Do not invent scores in this preflight.

| Criterion | Weight | Required measured evidence |
|---|---:|---|
